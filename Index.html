What You’ll Need for Home made Ice Cream

Grab these 5 ingredients and give it a go:

  • Whole Milk (the colder, the better, Do use whole milk for the creamiest results.)
  • Sugar — granulated sugar.
  • Heavy Cream ( the colder the better.)
  • Pure Peppermint Extract — feel free to sub with another extract flavor, like vanilla if you wish.
  • Semi-sweet Chocolate Bar, chopped into bits (you’ll need a 4oz bar, or you can use chopped-up chocolate chips.)

    • The most inportant thing you will need is a Ice-cream Maker

    Instructions

      1. In a bowl, use whisk to combine milk and sugar until dissolved.

      2. Stir in heavy cream and peppermint.

      3. Turn ice cream maker on and pour mixture in and let churn for about 25 min.

      4. During the last 5 minutes of churning, slowly add in chopped chocolate.

      5. Transfer ice cream in airtight container for at least 2-3 hours before enjoying it.

    Facts about Ice cream

    While you wait for your ice cream to freeze you can read a few facts about the history of ice cream. Ice cream's origins date back to ancient times, appearing around 2000 B.C.E. in Mesopotamia and 700 B.C.E. in China. During the Tang Dynasty, a recipe involving a mixture of snow, milk, and rice was created for the nobility. Rome (c. 1st Century C.E.): Emperor Nero sent runners to the Apennine mountains for ice, which was then flavored with fruit juices and honey.
